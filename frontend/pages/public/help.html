Help Centre & FAQs

Quick answers about bookings, availability, directions and listings.

What is AFCON PAMOJA?

An independent platform by Alimap Incorporation that brings football information, travel planning and local services together. It is not an official tournament, CAF or government service.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse everything without an account. You’ll only need one to track enquiries and bookings later.

Is a booking request the same as a confirmed booking?

No. A request or enquiry goes to the provider. A booking is confirmed only when the provider confirms it and any payment step is complete.

How current is availability?

Availability is entered by each provider and shown with when it was last updated. If it looks old, confirm with the provider before you plan around it.

What does “verified” mean?

Alimap has checked evidence for the business. Listings without that check are labelled so, and sample or placeholder content is marked clearly.

How do directions work?

“Get directions” opens Google Maps with the destination filled in. Maps estimates are not guaranteed, so allow extra time on match days.

Is the AI assistant a person?

No. It’s an automated assistant that only uses platform data. You can contact a person at any time from the Contact page.

Does AFCON PAMOJA sell official tickets?

No. We don’t sell official tickets or grant stadium access. Use the official channels announced by the organisers.

How do I list my business?

Alimap creates and verifies business profiles, then sends you a secure activation link. Use the Contact page to start.

I found a mistake in a listing. What should I do?

Use “Report an incorrect location or detail” on the listing page, or contact support. We’ll check with the provider.
