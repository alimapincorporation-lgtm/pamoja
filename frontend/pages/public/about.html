About AFCON PAMOJA

An independent platform for football fans, travellers and local businesses in Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania.

What it is

AFCON PAMOJA brings football information, travel planning and local services together in one place. Visitors can find hotels, tours, transport, food and stadium information, then contact providers or request bookings.

Independent platform

AFCON PAMOJA is built and operated by Alimap Incorporation. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or licensed by CAF, AFCON, any organising committee, stadium operator or government body. Official names, logos, fixtures and media appear only where written permission has been given.

What we promise

  • Labels for what is verified, estimated, sponsored or out of date.
  • Directions that open in Google Maps from locations Alimap has checked.
  • Recommendations that never invent availability, prices or bookings.
  • A way to report mistakes and reach a person.

How businesses join

  1. Alimap makes contact

    We explain the platform, fees and responsibilities.

  2. We create and check the profile

    Details, location and evidence are recorded. “Verified” is shown only after checks are done.

  3. You receive a secure invitation

    You set your own password and review your listing.

  4. You manage your listing

    Update services, prices, hours and availability yourself.
