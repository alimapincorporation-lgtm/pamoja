Privacy notice

Draft for legal review.

Draft outline for review. This is not final legal text. Alimap should have local counsel review it for Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania before launch.

What we collect

Details you give us, such as name, email and the content of enquiries, plus basic usage data. We aim to collect only what is needed.

How we use it

To pass your enquiries and requests to providers, provide your account, keep the platform secure and improve it.

Who sees it

The provider you contact sees your enquiry. Alimap staff see only what their role needs.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and to stop messages. Contact support to make a request.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed. Retention periods will be set before launch.

Contact

Use the Contact page to reach the team.
